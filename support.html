Volume Mixer Bar

Support

Questions, bugs or ideas: open an issue at github.com/danielacorner/volume-mixer-bar/issues. We reply within a few days.

Common questions

Where is the app?

In the menu bar, near the clock: a sliders icon. There is no Dock icon and no window.

macOS asks to allow "System Audio Recording". Why?

That is how macOS lets an app change another app's volume. The sound is only turned up or down and passed straight to your speakers; nothing is recorded or saved. If you clicked Don't Allow, turn Volume Mixer Bar on in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording.

An app is missing from the list.

Apps appear once they have used sound. Start playing something in the app and it shows up within a second. A green dot marks the apps playing right now.

Can I control notification sounds per app?

macOS plays the sound of most notifications itself, for every app, so they share one "Notification sounds" slider. Apps that play their own notification sound (many chat apps, and web notifications in browsers) follow that app's slider.

I switched to headphones and it still works?

Yes. The mixer follows the output device you pick in macOS.

How do I start it at login?

Tick "Open at login" at the bottom of the mixer.

How do I quit it?

Click Quit at the bottom of the mixer. Every app goes back to its normal volume.

Privacy

Volume Mixer Bar collects nothing and never connects to the internet. Privacy policy.
