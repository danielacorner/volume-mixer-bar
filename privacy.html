Volume Mixer Bar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Volume Mixer Bar collects no data. Nothing leaves your Mac.

Audio access

When you turn an app up or down, macOS asks you to allow audio access ("System Audio Recording"). Volume Mixer Bar uses it only to change the loudness of that app's sound as it plays and pass it straight on to your speakers or headphones. The sound is never recorded, saved to disk, analysed or sent anywhere.

What the app reads

The names and icons of the apps that use audio, so it can list them. This stays in memory on your Mac while the app runs.

What the app stores

Only your settings (each app's volume and mute), in the app's own preferences on your Mac.

What the app does not do

  • No network access: it does not connect to the internet.
  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash reporting.
  • No sharing with third parties.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.
